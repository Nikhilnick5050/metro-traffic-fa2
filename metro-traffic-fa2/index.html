Solving Metro Traffic Congestion

Using GPS and data models to find hotspots and suggest alternate routes in Delhi & Bengaluru.

Nikhil Shinde

Problem Empathize Define Ideate Prototype Test

1. The Problem

Metro cities like Delhi and Bengaluru face severe traffic congestion. Today, authorities still rely heavily on manual surveys to understand traffic — which is slow, expensive, and often outdated.

This leads to delayed decisions, poor route planning, and frustrated commuters stuck in jams every day.

2. Empathize

Commuters

Lose hours daily in traffic, miss work and appointments, and feel stressed.

Traffic Police

Depend on manual counts and guesswork to manage junctions and diversions.

City Planners

Need real data to design better roads, signals, and public transport.

3. Define

"How might we use GPS and data models to automatically detect traffic hotspots and suggest alternate routes, so commuters and planners can act faster?"

4. Ideate

  • Collect live GPS data from vehicles and phones.
  • Build a data model that spots congestion hotspots.
  • Suggest alternate, less-crowded routes in real time.
  • Show a simple dashboard for planners and commuters.

5. Prototype

A lightweight web dashboard that:

  • Marks congestion hotspots on a city map.
  • Ranks routes by current traffic load.
  • Recommends the fastest alternate route.

6. Test & Feedback

Show the prototype to a few commuters and a traffic volunteer. Collect feedback on clarity, usefulness, and ease of use, then refine the dashboard.
